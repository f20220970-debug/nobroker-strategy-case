# Growth opportunities, conversion and category expansion

## The opportunity in one line
In FY24, subscriptions were **99%** of NoBroker's **INR 803 Cr** operating revenue (Entrackr, from RoC filings), and the
company has said it is pushing home services and resale. Every rental or purchase is a **move event**, the moment a
household needs movers, an agreement, cleaning, painting, a loan or insurance. Earning more from that moment, per
customer, is the biggest lever.

## The funnel model (`model/growth_model.xlsx`, illustrative inputs)
Visitors → leads (contact an owner) → paid plans; leads who move → services attached × order value × take rate.
With illustrative inputs for one city (400,000 monthly visitors, 12% become leads, 6% of leads pay, 35% of leads move
that month):
- **48,000 leads, 2,880 paid plans and 16,800 move events a month.**
- Services could earn about **INR 1.6 Cr a month vs INR 0.7 Cr from plans**, around 69% of revenue, if attach rates
  like 18% for packers & movers and 30% for rental agreements were reached.
- **Revenue per lead** is the number to grow: about INR 490 in this illustration.
- The sheet's sensitivity rows show what +5 points of movers attach, or +1 point of lead-to-paid conversion, is worth.

These are assumptions to show the structure and the levers, not NoBroker's real figures.

## Conversion optimisation: where to test first
| Funnel step | Idea | Metric | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead → paid plan | Show the brokerage saved ("you save ~INR 40,000") at the paywall | Lead-to-paid rate | Refund requests |
| Paid → move | Offer a "move-in bundle" (agreement + movers + cleaning) at a single price | Services per move event | Bundle discount cost |
| Move → next service | Day-30 nudge for painting, insurance, broadband | Second-service rate in 60 days | Unsubscribes |

## Category expansion: which new categories first (`Category priority` sheet)
Weighted scores on market size (25%), tie to the move moment (25%), margin (20%), ease of operations (15%) and
trust fit (15%). Scores are my judgement calls; change them in the sheet.

| Rank | Category | Score | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Tenant verification & rent guarantee** | 4.15 | Sits exactly at signing; protects owners, builds trust; software-heavy, so easy to run |
| 2 | **Home insurance (renters and owners)** | 4.10 | High margin, no operations, a natural add-on to agreements and loans |
| 3 | **Furniture & appliance rental** | 3.90 | Big need at move-in for young renters; operationally heavier |
| 4 | Resale home inspection & legal check | 3.75 | Fits the resale push; builds buyer trust |
| 5 | Broadband & utilities setup | 3.60 | Strong move-in fit, low margin |
| 6 | Interiors & modular kitchens | 3.35 | Large tickets but long cycles and heavy operations |
| 7 | Property management for NRIs | 3.10 | Valuable but a separate audience |

**Recommendation:** pilot **tenant verification with a rent guarantee** and **renters' insurance** together at the
agreement step in one city: both are low-operations, high-trust add-ons sold at the moment of highest intent. Measure
attach rate, revenue per move event and owner satisfaction against a control city.
