# NoBroker Strategy Case: Competitive Benchmarking, Growth and Category Expansion

A business strategy case on NoBroker, India's broker-free property platform, built from public information.

- **[Competitive benchmarking and positioning](docs/competitive_benchmarking.md):** NoBroker vs 99acres, MagicBricks,
  Housing.com, Square Yards and local brokers; a positioning map; where inbound leads leak; six ideas to increase
  inbound leads, each with a metric.
- **[Growth, conversion and category expansion](docs/growth_and_category_expansion.md):** the move-event opportunity,
  a funnel and cross-sell model, conversion tests by funnel step, and a weighted ranking of new categories.
- **[Model (Excel)](model/growth_model.xlsx):** formula-driven funnel and service-attach revenue model with
  sensitivities, plus the category scoring sheet. Inputs are illustrative and editable.
- **Related:** [Hyderabad housing analytics](https://github.com/f20220970-debug/hyderabad-housing-analytics):
  resale price prediction and locality clusters with supply gaps.

## The story in three lines
1. In FY24, subscriptions were 99% of NoBroker's INR 803 Cr operating revenue (Entrackr, from RoC filings).
2. Every rental or purchase is a move event; services attached to it are the biggest lever on revenue per customer.
3. Start with low-operations, high-trust add-ons at the agreement step (tenant verification with rent guarantee,
   renters' insurance), and grow leads where supply is thin with owner acquisition and a free price-estimate tool.
